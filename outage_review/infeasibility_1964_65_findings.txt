Interpretation and limits
-------------------------
This proves an export-precision artifact in these saved diagnostics. It does
not prove which constraints made the original complete live model fail.
The .ilp omits the objective and constraints removed during the time-limited
conflict search. It cannot substitute for the complete failed model.
The existing network files are inputs saved before rolling dispatch; they do
not preserve all storage states and resource counters carried into window 67.

Annual fuel depletion remains a hypothesis, not an identified cause. The
network household shares already sum to one; renormalizing those metadata
shares is not an established remedy for the original failure.
