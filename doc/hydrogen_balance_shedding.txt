Hydrogen shedding for process requirements
=========================================

The fuel-use campaign now enables a separate H2 balance slack at hydrogen buses
with consuming processes, including buses with no positive final hydrogen Load.
This covers Sabatier, methanolisation, Fischer-Tropsch and other H2-using Links.
Gas/oil deratings, existing synthesis minimum outputs, physical capacities and
storage assumptions are preserved. No minimum-dispatch cleanup is applied.

Configuration in config/config.fuel_use_disruption_2025.yaml:

disruption:
  load_shedding:
    fuel:
      enable: true
      default_cost: 23300
      hydrogen_balance:
        enable: true
        cost: null
        costs_by_bus: {}

With cost: null, price inheritance is costs_by_carrier.H2, then
costs_by_fuel.hydrogen, then the common default_cost. Therefore the present
hydrogen penalty is the same 23300 EUR/MWh as other fuel shedding. An explicit
hydrogen_balance.cost or costs_by_bus overrides that inherited price. Common
fuel regional_cost_adders are applied afterward. All final prices must be
positive and finite. Setting hydrogen_balance.enable: false removes this
additional slack; ordinary final-demand hydrogen shedding remains available.

Scope and bounds
----------------
One fixed Generator per H2 bus supplies missing hydrogen at the configured
penalty. Its capacity envelope is the connected process input capacity and
efficiencies. A model constraint additionally limits its dispatch to actual
hydrogen consumption by those processes at every snapshot. It cannot create
hydrogen solely for stock filling or export when processes are not consuming.
Pure H2 transport/storage transfer Links are excluded from the requirement.
Secondary H2 inputs on multi-port Links are included. Continuous forward-only
consumption is required; ambiguous reverse consumption raises an explicit error.

This is a missing-H2-supply proxy: the synthesis process can continue operating
using penalized, otherwise unavailable hydrogen. It is distinct from reducing
industrial production. No upstream energy use/emissions are modelled for this
artificial hydrogen, and it does not consume the gas resource allowance.

Existing Load-bounded fuel shedding is retained, including final hydrogen
Loads. It is not duplicated: the new slack is bounded by process consumption,
and final-demand slack is bounded by final Load. The two quantities are
reported separately. Combined slack cannot exceed those two requirements.

Reports per case
----------------
- hydrogen_shedding_by_bus.csv: missing H2 MWh, peak MW, hours, actual process H2
  use, price and penalty cost, with bus/region/country labels.
- hydrogen_shedding_timeseries.csv: hourly missing H2 MW per balance Generator.
- dispatch_summary.csv: hydrogen_supply_shortfall_MWh, hydrogen_shedding_cost_EUR.
- Existing fuel_shedding files still measure prescribed final fuel demand only.

Rolling reports include only implemented hours, not lookahead/overlap twice.
Penalty costs are part of ordinary dispatch operating cost; do not add them to
that cost a second time. Heatmap costs excluding shedding subtract this penalty.

Rerunning
---------
Copy the cumulative update into the repository on Sophia. To retry task 17
(1988-1989, gas_reduction_90) using the unchanged 28-task winter mapping:

  sbatch --array=17 run_fuel_use_winter_test_2025.sbatch

To rerun all control/disruption cases consistently:

  sbatch run_fuel_use_winter_test_2025.sbatch

The solve driver refreshes H2 balance shedding from the current configuration,
even if an older prepared network is reused with mtime-only rerun triggers.
New named report outputs ensure completed older cases are scheduled again.
To explicitly recreate preparation as well, append:

  --forcerun prepare_disruption_network apply_interconnector_disruption solve_disruption_dispatch

Local verification
------------------
The captured failed full-precision window was replayed locally with all original
minimum-output requirements and capacities preserved. Adding H2 slacks with
actual-consumption bounds made the full LP feasible at tolerance 1e-7 MW. This
was a feasibility-only check; the economic objective was zeroed for that check.
Separate solved tests verify the real penalty, price inheritance, storage-use
bounds, secondary inputs, final-demand separation and rolling accounting.
No Sophia folders were accessed during this implementation; the replay uses
the previously copied local captures.
